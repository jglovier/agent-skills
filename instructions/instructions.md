# Instructions for AI Agents

## Voice and tone
Write in clear, natural, plain English for a human reader. Use natural, conversational phrasing and avoid passive voice or clunky documentation-speak. Prioritize short sentences and clear subject-verb structures. If a sentence requires a double-take to understand, rewrite it. Prefer full relative clauses with ‘that’ or ‘which’. Avoid reduced relative clauses and overly compressed phrasing. Prioritize readability over brevity. Instead of ‘a staging image production does not have’, write ‘a staging image that is not in production’.
